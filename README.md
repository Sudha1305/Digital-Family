# 🌳 Digital Growing Family Tree

**Every Family Has a Story Worth Keeping.**

A simple, interactive web application to build a digital family tree, connect relatives, and preserve family memories.


## 🛠️ Tech Stack
HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · SVG · Local Storage

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Choose a family symbol.
3. Add your family details and start building your tree.

## 🔮 Future Enhancements
- Family photos and voice memories
- AI-powered family storytelling
- Family invitations and collaboration
- Cloud storage and synchronization


🌱 *Preserve your roots. Celebrate your memories. Keep your family story alive.*
